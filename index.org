* selected work

** total_perspective_vortex
:PROPERTIES:
:URL: https://github.com/erikandmitrievich/total_perspective_vortex
:META: EEG motor-imagery classification
:END:

Motor-imagery classification over PhysioNet's EEGMMIDB. Common Spatial Patterns written from scratch as scikit-learn transformer, feeding LDA; 0.63 mean held-out accuracy over 109 subjects and 6 binary task groups. All derivation (CSP + architecture) sit beside the implementation in docs/; CSP is checked against mne.decoding.CSP.

** audio_print
:PROPERTIES:
:URL: https://github.com/erikandmitrievich/audio_print
:META: audio fingerprinting in Julia
:END:

A Shazam-style audio identification engine: spectrogram analysis, spectral peak extraction, and combinatorial hashing matched against a SQLite fingerprint database. 97.8% identification on 138 noisy clips against an 8,000-song database, ~260 ms per query.

** transcendence
:PROPERTIES:
:URL: https://github.com/erikandmitrievich/transcendence
:META: full-stack web app, team of three
:END:

Browser Pong with: accounts, 2FA, tournaments system, three microservices behind a WAF, TLS and secrets issued by Vault. My part was game (with AI opponent that sees the board once per second and predicts where the ball lands), most of the frontend and half of the match service. Canvas engine from scratch, SPA hand-written in TS, no frontend framework. Final project of 42 Common Core.
